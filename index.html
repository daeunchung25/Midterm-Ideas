IDEA 02

How Much Is Your Time Worth?

An interactive website that helps people understand how much time their everyday digital habits actually take up. Users enter activities like TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, or gaming and how much time they spend on them each day. The website translates those small daily numbers into months, years, and portions of a lifetime, then helps users decide whether they want to keep that habit or use some of that time differently.

IDEA 03

Can I Afford This?

A website that makes the long-term impact of small repeated purchases easier to understand. Users enter something they regularly buy, its price, and how often they buy it. The website calculates the monthly and yearly cost and compares it with a personal savings goal, helping users decide whether the purchase is worth it to them.
