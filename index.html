MIDTERM PROJECT PROPOSALS

THREE
IDEAS.

Three interactive experiments exploring identity, time, money, and the ways digital tools can help us understand everyday choices differently.

IDEA 01

Personal Logo Generator

An interactive website that asks questions about a person's name, personality, and aesthetic, then turns those answers into a personalized visual identity. The project explores what happens when something as complex as identity is translated into a brand.

IDEA 02

How Much Is Your Time Worth?

Small amounts of time rarely feel significant in the moment. This website turns everyday digital habits into days and years, helping users visualize how their time accumulates and decide whether it reflects what they actually value.

TRY PROTOTYPE →
IDEA 03

Can I Afford This?

A tool that reveals the long-term impact of small, repeated purchases. Users compare everyday spending with something they personally want to save for, making an otherwise invisible financial tradeoff easier to understand.
